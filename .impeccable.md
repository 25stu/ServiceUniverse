## Design Context

### Users

ServiceUniverse is used by citizens who need to discover and complete municipal
services through one coherent web entry point. The starter interface is also used
by student developers during integration, so service ownership, readiness, and
navigation must remain easy to understand.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, and welcoming. The product should feel civic and dependable
without looking bureaucratic, corporate, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction

A bright civic-atlas aesthetic: warm paper neutrals, deep ink blue, restrained
provider-specific accents, strong editorial typography, and map-like lines that
suggest one connected city. Avoid generic dashboard cards, neon-on-dark styling,
glassmorphism, and decorative gradients.

### Design Principles

1. Present one city platform first; provider identities are secondary navigation.
2. Make the next action obvious in plain language.
3. Reuse the shared shell, tokens, and interaction patterns across every service.
4. Design for keyboard access, visible focus, high contrast, reduced motion, and
   responsive use from the start.
5. Show unavailable or unfinished services honestly without breaking navigation.
